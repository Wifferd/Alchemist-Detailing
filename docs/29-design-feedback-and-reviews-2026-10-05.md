# Design feedback, and the reviews decisions (Oct 5, 2026)

## Colour scheme

The owner likes the site but finds the colours "old and classy, not in a good way"; the first palette came from a ChatGPT draft. He wants the site to feel modern and attractive, and sent sample images (black satin with a luminous metallic gold logo; black marble; an ivory/off-white editorial look with thin gold hairlines and a black car).

**Done the same day (first pass, tokens only):** true black instead of warm charcoal-brown, neutral greys, near-white text instead of cream, a brighter champagne gold with a white highlight, the WebGL art shifted from amber-brown to black with champagne light, and the headline font changed from Cinzel (Roman serif) to Jost (geometric sans) for a cleaner, modern feel. The brand monogram and wordmark are unchanged.

**For the design phase (last, per the owner):** his photos and videos; the ivory "showroom" light look from his samples as a second theme; motion per doc 18. PROPOSED.
