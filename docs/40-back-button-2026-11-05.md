# 40. The Back button (asked Oct 27, recorded Nov 5, 2026)

Doc 41 kept this number for the Back button. It is on the design branch (`design-build-wip`), not on the live site yet: it goes live with the design (doc 39).

## What the owner asked (Oct 27)

In their words:

> "Make an easily accessible “Back” button on every single page, make it so the bar it sits on only appears when scrolling up or on the top of the page, the button should make the user go to the previous page wherever they clicked.
>
> Ex.
>
> Opened “interior” on home page, scroll for a while then scrolls up and presses “back” now he is at the place where he selected Interior."

## What was built: `b33be3d` [bk] (Oct 28)

- **Back is in the top bar, next to the menu, on every page**, the 404 page too.
- **It is the browser's own history.** The button, the browser's Back and the iPhone's back swipe all do the same thing, and Forward works too.
- **Each page is kept where it was left**, also across a reload and when the tab is put away. Back lands exactly there. The owner's example (home, Interior, scroll, Back) comes back with the Interior card in the same place, on a phone and on a computer.
- **FAQ answers that were open stay open** when the visitor comes back to the FAQ.
- **The bar slides away while scrolling down** and comes back on the first move up, even a slow one. It always shows at the top of a page, with the menu open, with keyboard focus in it, and through a page change. The Services chapter bar moves with it. Under reduced motion it doesn't slide.
- Page names stay centred at 360 to 440 px, and Back keeps room for its word.

## The defaults chosen

The request doesn't say what Back does when nothing of the site is behind it, so these were chosen and go to the owner (doc 39, question 19):

- **A page opened directly** (a shared link, a bookmark): Back goes to the top of home, so it never leaves the site.
- **Home opened directly:** Back is hidden; there is nothing behind it.
- **Two quick presses count as one.**
- **The 404 page:** its Back is a link to home until its script finds a page of the site behind it.

## Later fixes that touched it

- **`fdeeed7` [p07]:** a heading that takes the focus after Back or Forward shows no gold focus ring; real controls keep theirs.
- **`9e29390` [p09]:**
  - **No bar without a scroll up.** On a phone the bar came back at the end of the foam wash with no scroll up: the wash gives back its length with a scroll of its own, and the finger's last pixels landed just after it. The bar now leaves out the wash's own scrolls and counts only what the finger did. The wash is unchanged.
  - **Back closes a full-size photo.** With a photo open (the Gallery, or home's Our work strip), Back or the iPhone's swipe back moved the page behind and left the photo on top of a frozen page. Now the open photo is a Back step of its own: Back closes it with the page where it was, and the next Back leaves the page. The X, Esc and a tap beside the photo close it the same way, and Forward opens it again. A double press (a double-click on the X, two quick taps) makes only one Back, so the visitor stays on the page. The photo's link to the services goes there, and Back from the services returns to the photo's page. A reload with a photo open comes back without it.
  - **A shared link opens on its own page.** A link straight to a page (the FAQ, say) no longer shows Home's hero and the name "Home" first, so Back never shows beside "Home" there.
  - **One Back in the booking.** The booking's own step button now reads "Previous", so the screen no longer shows two buttons called Back. Its spoken label names the step it returns to (the step names don't fit beside Send Request on a 360 px phone). The bar's Back still leaves the booking.
  - **The 404 page has the bar**, with Back, "Page not found" in the middle and the AD mark, like every other page.
- **`1893144` [p10]:** the bar's "Back" now uses the bar's own expanded label face, and on phones Back is a little wider to hold it: 66 px up to 640 px wide, 62 px up to 400 px (84 px on a computer, unchanged).
- **Panel 11** (Nov 5): its fix was reverted (doc 39), so it changed nothing here. Two of its findings touch Back and stay open: Back then Forward from the booking receipt opens an empty step 01 instead of the receipt; and when the bar comes back mid-page, cut page text shows in the 12 px strip above it. A related question goes to the owner (doc 39, question 25): a half-filled booking doesn't survive a reload, because booking answers are kept only in memory.
